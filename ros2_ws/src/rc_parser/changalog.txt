QUY TAC valid
valid = manual.valid && data_source==SOURCE_RC && !manual_control_signal_lost, va con frame moi trong frame_timeout (0.2s). Mat RC -> valid=false -> kill/land = false.

BAN DO KENH TREN PI
ch[0..3] = roll, pitch, throttle, yaw; ch[4..9] = aux1..aux6; ch[10..15] = 1500.
Phan kenh TX: CH1-4 stick, CH5 mode (Pi khong thay), CH6 kill.

CAU HINH PX4 (lam khi co PX4)
- RC_MAP_KILL_SW = 6 va RC_MAP_AUX2 = 6 (ch[5]=aux2=kill, khop kill_channel=5, kill_switch_node khong sua)
- Hieu chinh radio khi cong tac kill o vi tri AN TOAN (thap); neu khong aux2 co the khong len +1
- Dat RC_FAILS_THR va failsafe throttle tren TX; test tat TX
- Gan mode switch (CH5), kiem tra land chua noi vao RC
